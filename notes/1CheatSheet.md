### Overfitting

* **Early Stopping**: A regularization technique that stops training when validation performance stops improving.

---
**Forward Propagation vs Backward Propagation**: 
1. Fwd propagation <mark> makes predictions </mark>
2. Bckwd Propagation <mark>learns from prediction error</mark> and updates model weights.

---
> <mark>**Vanishing gradient**</mark> is a training problem where gradients become extremely small during backpropagation,
> **causing early-layer weights to update very slowly or not at all**.
