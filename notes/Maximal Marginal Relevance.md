**Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR):**
is a **retrieval reranking strategy** that selects documents that are **relevant to the user query** while also **being different from the chunks already selected**, 
thereby reducing redundant or duplicate content.

It considers two things:
• Relevant to the query
• Different from the chunks already selected
